Field intel

What's waiting in Takistan

The map is 12.8 km of desert, mountains and burned-out towns. Here's what you'll run into out there.

THREAT // WILDLIFE

The wild hunts back

  • Lone tigers, panthers and cheetahs can turn up anywhere, even in town
  • Packs of big cats and rhinos roam the open country
  • Snakes, rats and ravens in the streets and fields
  • Wild camels and horse herds to hunt or tame
MOBILITY // RIDES

Find it, fix it, drive it

  • Aussiecrayz Doomsday cars spawn across the map, usually missing parts
  • 4KBOSS trucks and SUVs at the vehicle trader
  • RedFalcon helicopters for the brave
  • Saddle up horses and camels when the fuel runs out
ECONOMY // TRADE

A real trader economy

  • 18 trader posts, from food and ammo to helicopters
  • Bank, ATMs and a garage to store your vehicles
  • Sell pelts, heads and meat from the hunt
  • A Black Market for the rare stuff
SUPPLY // LOOT

Loot worth searching for

Ammo and food turn up often. Guns, car parts, building gear and medical supplies are a real find, so every house is worth checking.

COLLECT // RARE

Collectibles hunt

Nearly a thousand collectibles hide in the world: trading cards, Funko Pops, retro game cartridges, comics, statues, bottle caps and 80 teddy bears. Trade them at the Collectables trader.

HOLD // BASES

Build and hold

Base Building Plus, code locks and heavy-duty storage. Grow your own crops, or something stronger, with CannabisPlus and DrugsPLUS.

Grid reference · Know your ground

Trader posts, airfields and loot tiers

Positions are in-game X and Z coordinates in metres. Loot gets better the further north you push: you spawn in the south, so gear up on the way and hit the north when you're ready. How to join

  1. Open the DayZ launcher

    Use the official launcher or DZSA Launcher, whichever you normally play with.

  2. Find the server

    Search for "CruZaderZ" or use direct connect with 5.226.142.84 and port 2302.

  3. Let the mods download

    The server runs a large mod pack with vehicles, animals, weapons and more. The first download takes a while, so start it before you want to play.

  4. Check in on Discord

    Server rules, announcements, wipe news and help all live on the Discord.
